## General

### What is OmniSync?

OmniSync is a Shopify app that synchronizes your inventory, prices, and orders across multiple sales channels: Mercado Libre, WhatsApp Business, Amazon, and Instagram.

### Which channels are supported?

| Channel | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Mercado Libre | Available |
| WhatsApp Business | Available |
| Amazon | Available |
| Instagram Shopping | Available |

### Is OmniSync available on the Shopify App Store?

OmniSync is currently under review. Once approved, you'll be able to install it directly from the App Store.

## Installation

### What Shopify permissions does OmniSync need?

OmniSync requires read/write access to products, inventory, and price rules. It also needs read access to orders for cross-channel order management.

### Can I use OmniSync with multiple Shopify stores?

Each OmniSync installation is tied to a single Shopify store. For multiple stores, install the app separately on each.

## Pricing

### Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14 days of full access with no credit card required.

### How is billing handled?

All billing is processed through Shopify's native billing system. Charges appear on your regular Shopify invoice.

## Technical

### How often does inventory sync?

Sync intervals vary by plan: from 60 minutes (Free Trial) down to 1 minute (Enterprise). You can also trigger manual syncs at any time.

### What happens if a sync fails?

OmniSync logs all sync actions. Failed syncs are retried automatically. You can view detailed logs in the Sync Jobs section of the dashboard.

### Does OmniSync support multi-currency?

Yes. The Multi-currency price rule type allows you to set different prices per currency per channel, with configurable exchange rate margins.
