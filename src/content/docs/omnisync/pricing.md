## Price Protection

All plans include OmniSync's core price protection engine:

- **Per-channel locks**: Prevent accidental price overwrites
- **Price rule types**: Manual, Formula, Margin, Competitor-based, Cost-plus, Multi-currency, Price floor/ceiling, Scheduled
- **Smart alerts**: Get notified when prices fall outside configured ranges

## Billing

Billing is handled through Shopify's native billing API. Charges appear on your Shopify invoice.

## FAQ

**Can I switch plans?** Yes, at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

**Is there a free trial?** Yes — 14 days of full access. No credit card required.

**What happens when I exceed my product limit?** You'll receive a notification. Existing products stay synced; new products won't sync until you upgrade.
